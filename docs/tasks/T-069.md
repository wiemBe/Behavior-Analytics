## Build

1. **Provider** (base, REST and mock; read-only; `AQL_EXECUTION` required; MCP unsupported):
   `get_catchall_senders_keyed(*, devicetype_ids, window_start, window_end, bucket_seconds,
   max_rows) -> CatchallKeyedResult`. The AQL is T-063 §4's shape:
   - `SELECT logsourceid, logsourceidentifier AS "sender", starttime / {bucket_ms} AS "k",
     SUM(eventcount) AS "events", COUNT(*) AS "records" FROM events WHERE devicetype IN (…)`;
   - the half-open `starttime` bound;
   - `GROUP BY logsourceid, logsourceidentifier, starttime / {bucket_ms}`;
   - `ORDER BY SUM(eventcount) DESC LIMIT max_rows + 1`, and `self._ariel_scope(...)`.

   `devicetype_ids` are ints, validated before they enter the query. The window guard and the
   key and `logsourceid` guards are as in T-050a. The mock must have truncation and fault hooks.
2. **Settings** (bounded), in the WAF/IPS style:
   - `catchall_type_ids` (default `[67, 39, 212, 367, 4000]`, ints 1–100000, at most 32);
   - `catchall_max_new_senders_per_bucket` (20);
   - `catchall_max_active_senders_per_source` (2,000);
   - `catchall_min_admission_events` (3);
   - `catchall_sender_active_days`;
   - `catchall_new_sender_alerts_enabled` (**false**);
   - `catchall_top_senders` (20, at most 50);
   - retention days.
3. **Migration and models** (`app/models/catchall.py`).
   - Revision id `0020`. `down_revision` is **the head at the time you start** (run
     `alembic heads`, and say which in your report). The orchestrator re-chains at merge if needed.
   - `catchall_sender`: `(instance_id, catchall_log_source_id, identifier)` unique.
     - `identifier` is sanitized: printable characters only, stripped, at most 255. A value that
       fails is not a sender; its events are counted as untracked.
     - Also `first_seen_at`, `last_seen_at`, `events_24h` (nullable; recomputed per run from the
       stored buckets), `active` and `retired_at`.
     - `review_status` (`NEW` / `REVIEWED` / `DISMISSED`), `reviewed_by`, `reviewed_at`.
   - `catchall_bucket`: a hypertable, PK `(catchall_log_source_id, bucket_start)`.
     - `events`, `records` and `distinct_senders`, with `distinct_senders_lower_bound`;
     - `measurement` (`EXACT` / `LOWER_BOUND`);
     - `top_senders` JSONB, at most `catchall_top_senders` entries of `(identifier, events)`,
       only admitted or active identifiers;
     - the admission counts: `admitted`, `below_admission_threshold`, `rejected_over_quota`,
       `untracked_senders` and `untracked_events`;
     - `query_provenance` (§SC.5), `collected_at`.
   - The search family `CATCHALL_SENDERS` gets a seeded `search_cadence` row (default 1 h).
   - The migration must upgrade → downgrade → upgrade cleanly with no drift. The round-trip test
     is pinned to its own revision.
4. **Collector**, `app/collectors/catchall_collector.py` (plus a thin Celery task and a 60 s beat
   entry gated by `claim_slot(CATCHALL_SENDERS)`):
   - One keyed search per tick per instance over the configured types. Split on truncation, time
     first. At one bucket, a truncated result keeps its exact top and gives `LOWER_BOUND`.
     `FAILED` or `REJECTED` gives no bucket written. The watermark (`collection_watermark`,
     collector `catchall_senders`, one per instance) holds at the last contiguous fully collected
     bucket.
   - The search budget is `search_cadence_max_searches_per_tick`.
   - Admission per catch-all source per bucket, as §C.8. **A refused sender is never stored by
     name.**
   - **Bootstrap:** on the first successful run of an instance, every admitted sender is
     `REVIEWED`; after that a newly admitted sender is `NEW`. If
     `catchall_new_sender_alerts_enabled` is on, a NEW sender raises an inventory-class alert of
     subtype `NEW_CATCHALL_SENDER`, severity `LOW`, through `AlertService.apply_bulk`, with its
     own fingerprint (do not change existing fingerprints). It is off by default.
   - **Constant SQL per run**, with bulk upserts and loads. Pin it at two sizes (3 against 40
     senders).
   - Retention as WAF does.
5. **API**, `app/api/routes/catchall.py`, under `/api/v1/fleet/catchall`, read-guarded like
   `/fleet`:
   - `GET /senders`, filtered by `review_status`, `catchall_log_source_id` and `q` (literal
     `%`/`_`), paged, NEW first, then by `events_24h` descending;
   - `GET /buckets?catchall_log_source_id=&since=&until=`, bounded range;
   - `POST /senders/{id}/review` and `POST /senders/{id}/dismiss`. These need a mutating
     permission: use the one the fleet review (`POST /log-sources/{id}/review`) uses. Audit them,
     with the principal as actor. Add a `_BODIES` entry in `test_route_auth_guard.py` if
     needed, and change nothing else there.

## Tests (pin every guard and exit path)

- **Provider:** the AQL text (devicetype ints, key, filters, `LIMIT n+1`, padded scope); the
  guards; mock parity; MCP unsupported.
- **Fold and admission:**
  - sanitization (non-printable characters, length, empty);
  - quota, ceiling and threshold;
  - the deterministic order;
  - a refused sender is not stored;
  - the `top_senders` bound;
  - `LOWER_BOUND` propagation.
- **Collector:**
  - the bootstrap leaves no NEW sender;
  - after the bootstrap a sender is NEW;
  - alerts are off by default, and on by the setting;
  - a truncated wide search is split;
  - a failure writes no bucket and the watermark holds;
  - budget exhaustion;
  - a re-run is idempotent;
  - retention;
  - constant SQL at two sizes.
- **Gate:** one winner per slot.
- **API:** 401 / 403 / 404 / 422; the ordering; review and dismiss with audit.
- Migration round trip and drift.
- Break each guard on purpose, see a test fail, restore it.
