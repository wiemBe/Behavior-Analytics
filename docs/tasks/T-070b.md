## The question the view answers

**`/fleet/catchall`: "Which unconfigured devices are sending to the catch-all sources, and how
much?"**

1. **The senders queue**, first on the page.
   - Order: `NEW` first, then by `events_24h` descending, with unknown last. This is the API's
     order; do not re-sort it.
   - Columns:
     - the sender (`identifier`, monospace, never truncated without a full-value tooltip);
     - the catch-all source it arrived on;
     - first seen and last seen (UTC);
     - 24 h events, right-aligned with tabular figures. `null` is "unknown", never 0, and a
       tooltip says it is a lower bound from the stored top lists;
     - the review status;
     - who reviewed it and when;
     - actions.
   - Filters: review status (default **NEW**, with counts if the API gives them; otherwise none),
     catch-all source, and a search box sending `q` (debounced).
   - Actions: **Review** (marks it known) and **Dismiss** (not of interest). Both are audited
     server-side. They need `log_source:write`: without it, show the actions disabled with the
     reason, not hidden. Use optimistic UI only if a failure rolls back visibly. No double submit.
   - Explain once, in one sentence, what a sender is: the syslog hostname QRadar recorded
     (`logsourceidentifier`). It is often a relay, and it is a candidate unconfigured device, not
     a verdict.
2. **Per catch-all source, the volume and honesty over time.** Pick a source to see its buckets
   for a range of at most 7 days:
   - events per bucket, as a bar or step chart with the unit in the axis title (`events / 5 min`,
     using `bucket_seconds`) and UTC on the time axis;
   - distinct senders;
   - the measurement per bucket. `LOWER_BOUND` buckets are marked "at least", with a legend.
   - **A bucket with no row was not measured.** Draw it as a gap, labelled "not measured", never as
     0. Only buckets inside the range and before the latest measured bucket count as gaps. Do not
     invent a gap after the last collected bucket; say "collected up to <time UTC>" instead.
   - For the selected bucket: the top senders (at most 20) and the admission counts (`admitted`,
     `below_admission_threshold`, `rejected_over_quota`, `untracked_senders`, `untracked_events`),
     in words. Refused senders are counted and never stored by name; say so.
   - Where the list of catch-all sources comes from: see "Small backend addition" below.

**Nav:** add one entry, "Catch-all senders", under the fleet group after "Fleet sources". Update the
