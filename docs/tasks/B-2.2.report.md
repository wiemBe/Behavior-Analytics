## 1. Files changed

- `backend/app/repositories/anomaly.py` — added `latest_buckets()` (one `DISTINCT ON`
  statement, newest bucket per source, absent key = no bucket) and `baseline_cells()` (one
  row-value `IN` statement over `(log_source_id, weekday, hour)` keys); `latest_bucket()` and
  `baseline_cell()` are untouched and still serve the single-source detail route.
- `backend/app/api/routes/anomalies.py` — `list_source_behavior` now does one `latest_buckets`
  call plus one `baseline_cells` call instead of two statements per source; `anomaly_summary`
  takes `now = datetime.now(UTC)` once and makes one `baseline_cells` call for the fleet, so the
  weekday and hour can no longer come from different instants at a midnight boundary; `_source_behavior`
  populates the new field.
- `backend/app/schemas/anomaly.py` — added `SourceBehaviorOut.last_bucket_completeness:
  str | None = None` with a `#:` comment stating that `observed_eps` comes from this bucket and a
  non-`COMPLETE` value must not be presented as a measurement. No other response field changed.
- `frontend/src/lib/api.ts` — mirrored the field on `SourceBehavior` as optional
  `last_bucket_completeness?: "COMPLETE" | "PARTIAL" | "MISSING" | null`. No other frontend change.
- `backend/tests/integration/test_behavior_bulk_reads.py` (new) — statement-count, equivalence,
  completeness, summary and bulk-method tests as specified in the task.

  keep the single-source methods in place.

  `(isoweekday, hour)`, exactly as before; output order (`monitored_sources()` order) and every
  pre-existing field value are unchanged. `last_bucket_completeness` is `COMPLETE`, `PARTIAL` or

  zero median, open anomaly, buckets in different weekday-hour cells); `last_bucket_completeness`
  on three sources; summary counts over a mixed fleet; both bulk methods returning `{}` with zero
