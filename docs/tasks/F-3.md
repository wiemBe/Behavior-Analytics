   `detection_engineer` holding everything the `analyst` role holds plus `search:write` and
   `custom_rule:write`, and **no** `admin:*` permission. `admin` also gains `custom_rule:write`
   (it already holds `admin:*`; say whether that wildcard satisfies it, and add it explicitly if
   not). Seeding stays additive, as the file does today. Test what each of the four roles may
   and may not do on the new routes and on `POST/PATCH /searches`.

   `/ips` precedent), every route in §F.11's table: list, create, read, patch (with the version
   it was made against; 409 on conflict, the WAF-profile rule), delete (resolve the open alert,
   then delete, 204), stored-rule preview, draft preview, breaches (bounded).
   - Validation is F-1's pure function. A 422 lists every reason (the F-4 `reasons` shape).
   - The enabled-rule cap (`custom_rules_max_enabled`) is 409 naming the limit.
   - Each rule's response carries its status and reason, last evaluation time, the active
     breach, and whether alerting is in shadow mode (F-2's field).

   unmeasured counts, and the threshold with its unit. Constant SQL in the window's buckets
   (one bulk read, rule 9).
5. **Audit (§F.11).** `custom_rule.create | update | delete | enable | disable` with actor and
