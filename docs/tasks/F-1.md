## Build

1. **Enums** in `app/models/enums.py`: `CustomRuleSubjectKind` (`LOG_SOURCE`, `FLEET_CLASS`,
   `WAF_ENDPOINT`, `IPS_ENTITY`, `IPS_SOURCE`) and `CustomRuleMetric` (the ten metrics of
   §F.1.1). Do not change `AnomalyType` or any other existing enum (rule 6).
2. **Models and migration `0024_custom_rules`** (§F.3.1, §F.4.1, §F.4.3, §F.10):
   - the tables `custom_rule`, `custom_rule_breach` and `custom_rule_watermark`;
   - the subject-pairing `CHECK` (the `_IPS_SUBJECT_CHECK` pattern), the indexes and the partial
     unique index for one live breach per rule;
   - FKs that cascade the way §F.10 says;
   - additive only;
   - upgrade, `alembic check`, downgrade, upgrade and `alembic check` must all be clean;
   - a data-carrying round-trip integration test (the `0009` precedent).
3. **Catalog** `app/custom_rules/catalog.py` (§F.3.2). It is pure: one entry per metric, holding
   its subject kind, unit, the columns it reads, its measurement rule (`EXACT_ONLY` or
   `LOWER_BOUND_PROVES_INCREASE`) and its bulk loader key. Add a guard test as described in §F.14.
4. **Validation** (a pure function, used later by the API):
   - the subject-column pairing per kind;
   - the metric belongs to the kind;
   - ratio thresholds lie in (0, 1);
   - the operator set;
   - `sustain_buckets` and `recovery_buckets` are at least 1;
   - the enabled-rule cap, `custom_rules_max_enabled` (default 100).
5. **Bulk loaders** (§F.4.2): one statement per subject kind per tick, over the union of the
   subjects of that kind's enabled rules since the kind's watermark. **Constant SQL in the
   number of rules** (rule 9). Never a query per rule or per subject.
6. **The evaluator fold** `app/custom_rules/evaluate.py` (§F.5). It is pure and in memory:
   - sustain and recovery, as hysteresis;
   - unmeasured counts toward neither the breach nor the recovery;
   - LOWER_BOUND with GT or GE counts toward the breach and never resolves it;
   - LOWER_BOUND with LT or LE is INSUFFICIENT_DATA;
   - ratios need exact denominators, and an unmapped remainder makes the numerator a lower bound;
   - re-evaluation is idempotent;
   - the statuses `NOT_ENABLED`, `SUBJECT_RETIRED` and `DISABLED`, each with a reason.
7. **The beat task** `evaluate_custom_rules`, every 60 s, DB-only, sending no Ariel search
   (§F.4.1).
   - It is a no-op while `CUSTOM_RULES_ENABLED` is false, which is the default.
   - The first run seeds the watermark at the latest complete bucket and judges nothing.
   - `custom_rules_max_buckets_per_run` defaults to 288.
   - It writes the breach rows and the rule state.
   - It **does not open alerts.** Alerting is F-2.
   - Leave one clearly named seam (a callable or protocol, like `AlertEnqueuer` in
     `app/anomaly/engine.py`) where F-2 will plug in. Default it to no-op.
8. **Settings** in `app/core/config.py`:
   - `custom_rules_enabled` (false);
   - `custom_rules_max_enabled` (100);
   - `custom_rules_max_buckets_per_run` (288);
   - the retention settings of §F.10 (180 days, batch, max batches, interval) and the bounded
     daily retention task.

Not in this task: the API, permissions and audit (F-3), alerting (F-2), the preview (F-3), any
UI, and anything about F.2.
