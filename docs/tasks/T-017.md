## The decision (D-3, made by the orchestrator)

The request-count baseline and `WAF_REQUEST_RATE_SPIKE` drop a bucket whose **source-level**
endpoint coverage is below the floor. That coverage is `endpoint_coverage_ratio`: the share of the
source's events in the bucket that parsed to an endpoint at all.

The ratio paths check something else. Ratio training, the three ratio detectors and the
client-concentration detector check only the **breakdown's** coverage of the endpoint's *visible*
requests.

When unparseable requests differ in kind from parseable ones (malformed attack URLs are the
obvious case), the visible proportion is neither a lower nor an upper bound of the true one.
Under AGENTS.md rule 8 (fail closed), no verdict may rest on it.

**Decision:** the ratio and concentration paths also require
`endpoint_coverage_ratio >= waf_min_endpoint_coverage`, in training and in detection. This may be
relaxed later, but only with evidence from a live run.
