### ips_detection_detail whitelist
Top level of details: reason, baseline_low, baseline_high, threshold, sample_count. extra: robust_score_status, percentage_point_change, observed_measurement (EXACT/LOWER_BOUND only), absolute_delta, caveat, ratio, ratio_basis, robust_z, fallback_bound. extra.observed: events, max_distinct_ports, proportion, numerator, denominator. extra.expected: expected (routed to the field matching the observed shape only), completeness, baseline_version. Dropped: entity block, contributors (source IPs), queries/provenance, everything unknown.

### Contributor lists
No route exposes a contributor list. `ips_source_bucket.contributors` and `query_provenance` are deferred in every query; detection projection drops them. Reason text may name the entity (public by D.4).
