## Build

### 1. The engine (`app/ips/engine.py`, new): `IpsAnomalyEngine.evaluate_bucket(source, bucket)`

For one collected `IpsSourceBucket` of one source:
- **Subjects:**
  - every **admitted, active** `IpsEntity` of the source in the three entity dimensions
    (`DESTINATION_PORT`, `DESTINATION_IP`, `SIGNATURE`). Its count comes from its
    `IpsEntityMetric` row, or from the absence rule (`entity_count` in T-051a's
    `observe_entity`) when it has no row. Judging the absent entities lets an incident on a
    silent entity recover;
  - plus the source itself, for the four source detectors.
- **Load in a fixed number of statements:**
  - the bucket's facts;
  - the active entities;
  - their metric rows for the bucket;
  - the entity baseline cells for the bucket's weekday and hour;
  - the source baseline cells;
  - the detector states;
  - the live incidents;
  - and, for `IPS_NEW_DESTINATION_PORT`, the novelty facts:
    - the source's history buckets for `verify_port_history`, one bulk query over the history
      window;
    - the first-appearance bucket for `appearance_flood`;
    - the events since first appearance for the port entities inside the novelty window.
- **Run** each detector in `IPS_DETECTOR_FUNCTIONS` whose subject matches, with an
  `IpsDetectionContext` built from plain values:
  - map the settings onto `IpsThresholds` (add `ips_*` settings only where a threshold needs to
    be configurable, each with a one-line comment);
  - build each cell as an `IpsBaselineCell` with its `profile_version` and
    `normalizer_version`.
- **Lifecycle:** `app.anomaly.lifecycle` unchanged, with WAF's hysteresis (`open_after`,
  `resolve_after`).
  - On open, create an `IpsAnomaly` whose `fingerprint` is `ips_anomaly_fingerprint(...)`, set
    once and never changed.
  - Write the transitions and update the states.
  - Write everything in **one flush**.
- **No alert.** Do not call `AlertService`. T-052 adds alerting behind `ips_alerting_enabled`.
  Leave a short comment at the place WAF raises alerts, saying it is T-052's.
- **Statement count:** constant in the number of entities. Pin it at two sizes (for example 5 and
  200 entities, and with every detector enabled) in a new `tests/integration/test_ips_scale.py`,
  like `test_waf_scale.py`.

### 2. The pipeline step

After IPS collection for an instance, in `dispatch_ips_collection` or a service it calls, mirror
`waf_pipeline.py`. For each IPS source of the instance that has buckets:
1. **Evaluate** every collected, not-yet-evaluated bucket, oldest first, never beyond the
   source's collection watermark. Bound the run as WAF does, sized to the cadence. Advance the
   evaluation watermark bucket by bucket.
2. **Train** next. Call `IpsBaselineBuilder.rebuild_due(...)` with
   `train_through = the end of the last evaluated bucket`, bounded by
   `ips_baseline_rebuild_batch_size`.

The order is the point (WAF pipeline docstring): never train before evaluating. Load the
evaluation watermarks of all the instance's sources in **one** statement. A failure for one
source is recorded on its watermark (sanitized error, as WAF), leaves its watermarks where they
were, and does not stop the other sources.

## Tests

- **Engine (integration):**
  - a port-rate spike opens after `open_after` buckets and resolves after `resolve_after`;
  - an entity with no row and an exact, covered, untruncated dimension is judged as a measured
    zero, and an open incident on it can resolve;

  - a source detector (concentration) opens and resolves;

  - the per-run bound holds;
  - one source's failure leaves the others evaluated.

## Ruling 1 (orchestrator, 2026-10-14, after Codex stopped on a test conflict)

`tests/unit/test_detector_capabilities.py::test_every_non_operational_detector_states_its_limitation`
forbids an operational detector from carrying a `limitation`. That test is right and **stays
unchanged**. `limitation` means "what stops this detector running". Once the IPS detectors are
scheduled, **every IPS spec's `limitation` is `None`.** The per-source conditions are
**activation** conditions, not limitations, and they already sit in `activation`:
- `IPS_BLOCK_RATIO_SHIFT`: it needs an `ACTION` mapping with a value map;
- `IPS_SIGNATURE_BURST`: generic "Unknown …" names are refused.

Keep both in `activation`, and keep or sharpen their wording. Section 3's sentence about
"replace `_IPS_NO_SCHEDULE` with the real one where one remains" is superseded by this ruling.
`test_a_non_operational_detector_says_why` must still hold: when `ips_collection_enabled` is false
the IPS specs are `NOT_ENABLED`, and that test needs a reason. Supply it the way WAF supplies the
reason for a detector whose enabling setting is off, without putting a limitation on a running
detector.
