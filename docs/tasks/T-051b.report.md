## What I did

- Added entity and source weekday×hour baseline cells carrying profile and normalizer versions.
  Source ratio cells retain both per-bucket proportions and pooled numerator/denominator sums.
- Added typed IPS entity/source detector state and anomaly persistence. Partial unique indexes make
  nullable subjects unique per detector, and checks require exactly the subject matching
  `IPS_ENTITY` or `IPS_SOURCE`. Incident fingerprints are non-null and derive only from the stable
  subject and detector type.
- Reused `collection_watermark` for the future per-source IPS evaluation watermark, following WAF's
  `(instance_id, source-specific collector key)` pattern. No Phase A/WAF table or model changes are
  needed; T-051c will create/update the dynamic row when it adds the engine and pipeline.
- Kept all five new tables out of `HYPERTABLES`: baselines, current detector state and lifecycle
  incidents are aggregates/state, not append-only time-series samples. Existing
  `ips_source_bucket` and `ips_entity_metric` remain the IPS hypertables.
- Built entity training through `entity_count`, admitting only exact rows or provable sparse zeros.
  Maintenance, unevaluated buckets, width mismatches, version mismatches, lower bounds, unknowns,
  low coverage and incident spans are counted exclusions.
- Built exact source training for top-source share, block ratio and port fan-out. Block-ratio cells
  are omitted without a current action mapping and reject every bucket with unmapped actions.
- Bulk loaders fetch profiles/source buckets once and metric rows/incidents per configured chunk.
  The statement regression measured 5 statements for 10 entities and 14 for 200 entities at a
  50-entity chunk size: the 9-statement difference is exactly three statements for each of three
  additional chunks.
- Reused global `baseline_min_samples`, because IPS reliability has the same seasonal-cell meaning
  as Phase A/WAF; IPS has separate lookback and rebuild batch-size settings.
- Mutation check: temporarily disabled maintenance, `train_through`, bucket-width, version and
  incident-span guards together. The five corresponding cases failed (5 failed, 12 deselected),
  then the guards were restored and the complete suites passed.
