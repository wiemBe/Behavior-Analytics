## Build

1. **Navigation.** "Custom rules" → `/custom-rules` in the **Detection** group of
   `frontend/src/lib/nav.ts`, after Rules / Rule statistics (§F.12). Text only (D-11).
2. **`/custom-rules`, the list.** Answers "which of my rules is breaching or not being judged?".
   A table: name, subject (kind and its label), condition in words with its unit
   ("average EPS > 10 events/s for 2 buckets"), severity, enabled, status with its reason
   (`OK`, `OPEN`, `RECOVERING`, `NO_VERDICT`, `STALE`, `NOT_COLLECTED`, `SUBJECT_RETIRED`,
   `DISABLED`, `NOT_ENABLED`, and never-evaluated as its own state, not `OK`), last evaluated
   time with its zone. A page-level notice when evaluation is off (`evaluation_enabled` false,
   the setting named) and when alerting is in shadow mode (one plain sentence saying breaches are
   recorded and nothing pages until the setting is on). The latency statement (§F.12, verbatim
   meaning) once. A "New rule" action for callers with `custom_rule:write` only.
3. **`/custom-rules/new` and edit on `/custom-rules/[id]`, the editor** (§F.12 "The rule
   editor"; reuse the form patterns of the WAF/IPS mapping editors and `components/searches`):
   - the subject picker: kind first, then the matching picker. Log source and IPS source: a
     search over the existing source list API. Fleet class: the catalog's classes. WAF endpoint:
     choose a WAF source, then its admitted endpoints
     (`GET /behavior/waf/sources/{id}/endpoints`, paged). IPS entity: choose an IPS source, then
     `GET /behavior/ips/sources/{id}/entities`. An empty endpoint or entity list says why
     (only admitted endpoints/entities can be rule subjects; conditions the platform does not
     collect belong in a custom search) and links to `/searches/new`;
   - the metric picker from the catalog, filtered by kind, each with its unit and its note;
   - operator and threshold with the unit beside the field (a ratio is entered as a share in
     (0, 1) and labelled so); sustain and recovery steppers; the plain-language summary
     ("Fires after 2 consecutive buckets above 10 events/s; recovers after 2 buckets at or
     below"); severity; enabled;
   - the **live preview** beside the form: `POST /custom-rules/preview` with the draft,
     debounced, with an hours control (1–168 from the catalog). See "The preview chart".
   - Save: create, or PATCH with `expected_updated_at`. The 422 `reasons` render inline as a
     list, each reason as sent. The 409 cap names the limit and says what to do (disable
     another rule). The 409 version conflict says who changed it and offers to reload; it never
     overwrites silently.
4. **`/custom-rules/[id]`, the rule page.** Answers "is this rule firing, and what did it read?".
   Header: name, status with reason, enabled, alerting mode. The stored-rule preview chart
   (`GET /custom-rules/{id}/preview`, 24 h default, hours control). The active breach, if any,
   with worst and last value and their measurements, unmeasured buckets, and — in alerting mode
   — that an alert is open in `/alerts` (link to `/alerts`); in shadow mode, that nothing paged.
   The breach history table (`GET /custom-rules/{id}/breaches`). Edit, enable/disable and
   delete for writers; delete confirms in words that an open alert will be resolved.
   The subject links to its own page (`/behavior/sources/[id]`, the WAF source page, the IPS
   views), where investigation happens (§F.4.3).
5. **Permissions.** Without `custom_rule:write` the editor and the write actions are hidden, and
   the page says which roles can grant it (`admin`, `detection_engineer`). Reading stays.
   A route that `read:custom_rules` forbids renders the forbidden state, not an error.

## The preview chart (§F.7 — honesty is its contract)

Use the platform's chart component and tokens (`echarts`, the existing chart wrappers in
`components/`); no library-default style.
- Unmeasured buckets (`NOT_MEASURED`) are **gaps**, never zero, never bridged
  (`connectNulls: false`).
- A `LOWER_BOUND` bucket is marked and reads "at least n" in the tooltip and the text summary.
- The threshold is one labelled line with its unit; breach intervals are shaded bands.
- Beside the chart: "would have fired **N** times", measured and unmeasured counts; an
  unmeasured majority is said in words.
- Maintenance buckets are distinguishable and labelled.
- The y-axis carries the unit; times carry the zone; the chart has a text alternative.
